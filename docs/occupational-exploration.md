# Occupational exploration

Reviewed October 20, 2026. The shared occupation panel is rendered by `PathwayDetails`, so the same content is available on the homepage, selected onboarding result and dashboard trajectory explorer. It contains 24 occupation examples across the eight existing routes. It does not change scoring, saved diagnosis schemas, rankings or XP.

## Editorial method

Occupations and preparation summarize fields of practice, graduate competencies or curricula published by Salvadoran educational institutions. They are examples of destinations associated with a route, not a labor-demand survey or current vacancies. Occupation labels are normalized for student comprehension; suggested portfolio activities and self-employment exploration are Cumbreal's editorial guidance, not institutional endorsements or hiring requirements. Sources do not imply partnerships.

## Interpretation and boundaries

- Three examples per trajectory are illustrative, not exhaustive or ranked by individual employability. The panel follows the selected route, including for students who explore without a personal interest ranking.
- Preparation describes learning to pursue. Completing one starter project does not establish professional competence, a credential or permission to practice. Health examples explicitly require professional preparation and confirmation of practice requirements.
- No live job feed, salary or employment forecast, employer account, application submission, automatic CV generation or placement is implemented.
- Employment preparation and partnerships are documented as future phases in the README. Interests, self-reported abilities, submitted work and professionally reviewed evidence must remain distinguishable in any future CV.
- Refresh the sources before asserting current availability. The displayed consultation date is a review snapshot, not an automated freshness guarantee.
