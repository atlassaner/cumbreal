Before and after

What needed attention

  • The plain footer offered navigation but no clear next step or signature landscape.
  • The first new footer placed a small method link on a darker landscape area. Its measured contrast was too low; a cream-backed button now keeps it readable.
  • The hero's journey labels wrapped unevenly on phones. The final version aligns the three stages and keeps the decorative path away from them.
  • Pricing needed comparable scope, duration, coverage and actions without making proposed plans look available for checkout.

What changed

Colors and identity

The existing Editorial Cream & Cumbi direction remains: a school counselor's annotated atlas meets a mountain explorer's field notebook. The official logo, flat Cumbi artwork, Spanish interface, warm cream surfaces and restrained amber actions are preserved. The summit is bounded to the footer.

Typography and spacing

The original serif and body-font pairing remains. Prices now use the established size scale. The three journey stages stay readable on narrow screens, while pricing uses divided feature rows and aligned action buttons.

Layout

The new summit scene carries a useful exploration action and a cream glass navigation panel. The institutional page compares free prototype access, a proposed four-week pilot and a proposed monthly institutional plan. Existing limits, conditions and price hypotheses remain explicit.

Polish and interaction

The mobile menu closes with Escape and returns focus to its button. Links and controls retain visible focus treatment. Content appears immediately, and reduced-motion preferences are respected. Desktop and mobile receive separately compressed landscape assets.

Known limitations

  • Verification used desktop Microsoft Edge and emulated widths, not a physical phone or a full screen-reader audit. A narrower viewport approximated zoom reflow; native browser zoom and forced-colors behavior were not tested.
  • The summit is a still image. The separately scoped paid video render remains pending. No continuous decorative motion is presented as a finished video.
  • Institutional plans remain proposals; billing, a production institutional dashboard and job placement were not added.
  • This is a local build. No commit, push or production deployment was performed.
