Small links use deep teal #28697A, not #3B8078 on cream. Muted text #5A6B75 remains opaque. Faint #8B9CA6 is decorative only. Coral #D94828 and dark amber #C88515 cannot be small text on cream. Vibrant teal #4F9D97 is illustration/decorative fill only: white and ink both fail 4.5 on it. See V2-CONTRAST-RESULTS.md, generated by v2-contrast-stress.cjs. Do not apply opacity to ordinary text.

## Typography

DM Serif Display 400 for editorial headings, 1.15 leading and −0.025em tracking. Inter 400/500/600 for body, UI, and tabular data; no third font or synthesized serif bold. Data is the third role, using tabular Inter. Scale 12/14/16/18/24/30/36/48/64; body 16px at 1.6. Metadata 12px only when nonessential. Google Fonts: DM Serif Display and Inter, already installed via next/font. Remove undefined Playfair variable.

## Space, radius, depth

4px scale: 4/8/12/16/24/32/48/64/96. Balanced public page, denser product. Hero 64–96px desktop/40px mobile; process 64px, roadmap 80px; working panels 24px/16px. Radius: 8px controls, 12px panels, 16px composed containers; circles only for actual circular shapes. Borders and tinted surfaces are the primary depth cue. Card shadow 0 2px 12px rgb(34 49 58 / .05); elevated 0 10px 30px rgb(34 49 58 / .08); amber-glow 0 4px 20px rgb(242 170 46 / .25), reserved for achievement artwork. Selection uses border plus tint, never border+ring+shadow. Focus is an independent offset outline.

## Texture

Editorial flatness: cream planes, fine rules, mountain silhouettes and ruled evidence rows. No radial blobs, grid overlays, floating particles, or heavy black boxes.

## Motion and signature

180ms color/opacity feedback; readable content on first paint. No scroll entrances. Signature: Cumbi makes one small joyful jump after onboarding completion or a newly verified evidence record; not on ordinary route load. Reduced-motion removes transforms/jumps and presents final state immediately. Career hover uses color/border feedback rather than layout shifts. Aperture remains as an internal skills visual, not the logo.

Cumbi source reference is absent from all paths stated in V2, Downloads, and the Antigravity filename search. Implement an original code-native mountain explorer SVG with Curioso/Leyendo/Explorando/Celebrando states; do not claim fidelity to unseen artwork. A geometric teal silhouette provides the compact logo.

Don't: speculative personal match percentages; fake OAuth/reset delivery; invented student identities; animation cascades; broad transitions or stacked selection cues.
