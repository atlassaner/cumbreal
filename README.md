# New Hemisphere Learning: v0.1 - Making Learning Fun
What's NHL (New Hemisphere Learning)? NHL is a project I started as of 18/01/2026.
It is destined to facilitate the visualization of your academical progress in real time. 
As of 18/01/2026, it is in its earliest version possible, v0.1, which will serve to bridge the gap between conceptuality and realness.

---

New Hemisphere Learning directly addresses how us, as students, can often feel lost. When it comes to studying, especially in my country, El Salvador, it seems as if we are unable actually visualize our progress, losing time trying to assess where we stand and where we're heading. 

With this lack of a rewarding system, and treating studying as a chore rather than a conscious, hobby-esque decision, we oftentimes don't take studying seriously or feel like it's inherently stressful. With NHL, we're tackling down this problem.

By illustrating your progress to real academic achievements, we bridge the gap with what we think we are, what we show to be, and what we can be. This way, you can visualize your progress without having to assess it yourself every single time you want to check where you are. With NHL, you can link your Classroom account, Khan Academy, Coursera, and up to 30+ learning/education platforms. When you progress in your courses, when you get good grades, or when you turn in your homework on time, you'll level up. NHL is a gamified experience, where you have your own custom character that will grow when you're growing as a professional.

This way, your progress won't be only provable in certificates, but you can also monitor where you're standing as a student, what can you do to progress, and even get real-time, personalizable feedback from our AI! To actually prove you're learning and not just farming your character, our AI will sporadically generate flashcards and mini tests for you to prove that your abilities are kept honed, no matter how much time has passed ever since you progressed with one ability/course, our AI will ask you nonetheless. Go back to your sporadic AI quizzes idea, but make it smarter:

Every X levels, mandatory skill check
Can't progress past certain milestones without passing
Mix in spaced repetition (test old topics randomly)
Frame it positively: "Unlock new character customization by proving mastery"

You can also visualize where you stand not only by increasing levels, but also by getting rough, estimated feedback from our AIs, where you will be given levels for your abilities/subjects you have studied that are analagous to the CEFR framework! With all your abilities, you can even see what kind of student you are [archetype + exemplification: "You are undergraduate level"].

Furthermore, we will add rewards once this acquires investments from non-governmental organizations or governmental organizations. Per progress in validated courses and real assessment of learning, you will unlock points to exchange for rewards!

NHL will be entirely in Spanish, with AI-generated feedback in Spanish, making world-class learning resources accessible to Salvadoran students for the first time.

As this project will continue to be updated, we will add more and more features to make it as enjoying as possible.
