# Ascendra: v0.1 - Making Learning Fun
What's Ascendra? Ascendra is a project I started as of 18/01/2026.
It is destined to facilitate the visualization of your academical progress in real time. 
As of 18/01/2026, it is in its earliest version possible, v0.1, which will serve to bridge the gap between conceptuality and realness.

---

Ascendra directly addresses how us, as students, can often feel lost. When it comes to studying, especially in my country, El Salvador, it seems as if we are unable actually visualize our progress, losing time trying to assess where we stand and where we're heading. With this lack of a rewarding system, and treating studying as a chore rather than a conscious, hobby-esque decision, we oftentimes don't take studying seriously or feel like it's inherently stressful.
With Ascendra, we're tackling down this problem. By illustrating your progress to real academic achievements, we bridge the gap with what we think we are, what we show to be, and what we can be.
This way, you can visualize your progress without having to assess it yourself every single time you want to check where you are. 

As this project will continue to be updated, we will add more and more features to make it as enjoying as possible.
